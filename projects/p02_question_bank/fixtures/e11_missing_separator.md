# Options have no separator between the option text and its marker

## 3. Practice Questions

### Q1. (Multiple Choice)
What must stand between an option's text and its marker?

- A) Nothing **Incorrect.** The parser needs a separator.
- B) A spaced hyphen **Correct answer.** This line omits it, so it is not an option.
- C) A colon **Incorrect.** Not part of the format.

- **Answer:** B
